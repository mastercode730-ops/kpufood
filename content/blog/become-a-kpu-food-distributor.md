Regional food sells best when it comes with a story behind it. Shoppers who would walk past another generic label will stop for buransh squash, bamboo shoot pickle or amla murabba from the Kangra valley. If you already stock FMCG, food or gifting lines, our range will sit comfortably beside them.

## Who we work with

- **Distributors and super-stockists**, appointed area by area
- **Retail stores and supermarkets** across Himachal Pradesh
- **Hotels, homestays, restaurants and cafés** that buy in bulk packs
- **Gifting and festival counters** that need seasonal combinations

## What we offer trade partners

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Territory** | Appointed area-wise |
| **Packs** | Retail, bulk and institutional |
| **Gifting** | Festival combinations |
| **Private label** | Available on confirmed volume |

Trade prices stay steady through the year. We set margins so that the shelf price stays realistic for a Himachali household, which helps stock keep moving.

## Where we already supply

From our unit in Nagrota Bagwan, we already supply shops, hotels and distributors across Kangra district and beyond. That includes Dharamshala, McLeod Ganj, Palampur, Baijnath, Kangra town, Jawali, Dehra, Nurpur and many smaller towns, as well as Una, Hamirpur, Mandi, Chamba and Shimla.

Outside Himachal Pradesh, we support distribution to **Punjab, Haryana, Delhi NCR, Chandigarh and Uttarakhand** for confirmed bulk orders. Goods travel from Kangra by regular parcel and freight services.

## How to apply

Contact us by phone, on WhatsApp or through the [enquiry form](/#trade), and tell us:

1. **Your city or area**: the territory you want to cover
2. **Your current product lines**: what you already distribute or stock
3. **Your expected monthly volume**: even a rough figure helps

Our team will reply with **pack sizes, margins and dispatch schedules** for your area.
