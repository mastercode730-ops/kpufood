Most packaged food is made whenever a factory has spare capacity. Our range works the other way round. Each product is made in the weeks when its main ingredient is ready in the Kangra valley, then stocked for the rest of the year. If you know that calendar, you know when stock is freshest and when to place a large order.

## The year at a glance

| Product | Made in | Why then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buransh (rhododendron) squash | March – April | The flowers bloom for only a few weeks in spring |
| Mango pickle | May – July | Raw mangoes are firm and sour before they ripen |
| Jamun chutney | June – August | Jamun fruit comes in with the early monsoon |
| Bamboo shoot pickle | July – September | The monsoon pushes up tender new shoots |
| Mixed fruit jam | April – October | Follows the orchard fruit through summer |
| Red chilli chukh | October – December | Chillies are harvested and dried after the rains |
| Amla candy & murabba | November – January | Amla is ready after the first cold nights |
| Brahmi sharbat | All year | Brahmi leaf is available through the seasons |

## Why we don't make things out of season

Fruit picked in its season tastes better and needs less correcting with sugar, salt or colour. A mango pickle made from firm, sour green mangoes holds its bite in the oil for months. Buransh squash pressed from fresh blossoms has a colour and floral note that no flavouring can copy. Working this way is slower, but it is the reason the jar tastes the same as the one you bought last year.

## What this means if you are ordering

**For households:** every product is available all year from stock. What you buy in March was made when its fruit was at its best.

**For shops and distributors:** demand peaks around festivals, so plan bigger orders a few weeks ahead. Seasonal products are made in season and held in stock, so you won't be left waiting when the festival rush starts.

**For gift and festival packs:** tell us your dates early. We can then put together combinations from what is in stock, such as pickles, jam, squash and amla candy.

## How long does stock keep?

Pickles, chutneys and murabba are packed in food-grade jars and usually keep for 9 to 12 months. Squash, sharbat and juices are bottled to keep for 6 to 9 months. Store all of them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
