A jar of pickle often sits in the fridge door and comes out only with dal-chawal. Hill condiments are more useful than that. Here are six ways we use our own range at home, one for each of our most popular products.

## 1. Red chilli chukh: a spoon goes a long way

Chukh is hot, sour and sharp, so use it like a seasoning rather than a side dish.

- Stir a spoonful into a bowl of curd for an instant dip.
- Spread a thin layer on a hot paratha before rolling it up.
- Add half a spoon to a plain dal just before serving.

## 2. Buransh squash: one part to four

Rhododendron squash is a concentrate, so dilute it before drinking. Mix **one part squash with four parts chilled water**, add ice and squeeze in some lemon. On a summer afternoon, few drinks are as cooling.

## 3. Mixed fruit jam: more than toast

Our jam is cooked slowly until it sets, so it is thick and fruity. Try it:

- folded through a bowl of curd as a quick dessert,
- on pancakes or cheela,
- as a filling for hill-style sweet buns.

## 4. Amla candy: after a meal or on the road

Many people take amla candy as a digestive after a heavy meal. It also travels well, so keep a pouch in the car for long drives through the hills.

## 5. Bamboo shoot pickle with rajma-chawal

Bamboo pickle has a strong, earthy flavour that goes well with rice and rajma. You only need a little on the side of the plate, because a small amount brings out the whole meal.

## 6. Jamun chutney with grilled food

The tart, fruity jamun chutney cuts through rich food. Serve it with grilled or tandoori dishes, kebabs or paneer tikka, much as you would serve a mint chutney.

## A note on storage

To keep a jar at its best, always use a clean, dry spoon and close the lid tightly after use. Keep jars and bottles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
